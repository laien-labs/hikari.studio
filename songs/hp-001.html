I remembered the tone of my voice
it was a little higher
without you there

There are parts of yourself you can lose without noticing. One day the face in the mirror is still yours, but the smile feels rehearsed and the voice comes out in a shape you don’t remember choosing. You try it again in an empty room, listening for the moment it sounds like your own.
